  • Press F next to anything to become it: a couch, a toilet, a rug, a golden carrot, even a tree. Small things are fast, big things are slow.
  • Stand still and you're invisible. If anyone sees a couch move, they come straight for you.
  • Disguises last 10 seconds, then wear off with a poof and recharge while you're a bunny.
  • Your bag starts with room for 5 things. Drop loot in the burrow outside the back door, then hold E there to escape.
  • Spend your haul in the shop on a bigger backpack, faster paws, longer disguises and the Human Disguise perk.
  • Some of the family is asleep. Sprinting nearby wakes them, and sleepers get up on their own as the night goes on.
